## 1. Direction

A private nutrition practice: the order and credibility of a clinic, with the warmth of real food.
- **Structure (clinical):** cream paper grounds, hairline rules, directory-style rows instead of card grids, a numbered protocol, a "who does what" table for the medical part, a CV list and service fiches.
- **Character (warm):** a serif with italic accent words, soft pastel tints per service, real fruit cut-outs with real shadows, pill buttons, one sage band per page with food on it, and the featured plan in sage with a sun-yellow button.

What it rules out: saturated full-bleed colour fields, tilted market tags, eyebrow or kicker labels, gradient text, stock "dietitian" photos, scales, measuring tapes, before/after images, and pills or syringes anywhere.

## 2. Signature elements (use only these, in these places)

1. **Italic accent:** `<em>` inside an h1 or h2 wraps the last 2–4 words of the heading when it reads naturally. It is rendered as Fraunces italic in `--brand`, or `--sun` on the brand band. Use at most one per heading and never change the words.
2. **Still life** (`.still`, `.still__obj`): transparent PNG cut-outs with the CSS double drop-shadow.
   - Allowed only in: the home hero (3 objects around the portrait), the service heroes (2), the hero asides of servicios, cómo funciona, sobre mí, blog, contacto and 404 (2 each), the service-row thumbnails (1 per row) and the closing brand band (2).
   - Never on: the medical page, programas, FAQ, legal pages, the article, or anywhere next to medical content.
   - Hero objects settle once on load (`.still--settle`). With reduced motion there is no animation.
   - Colour rule: on the sage band and next to peach tints, avoid two orange or pink fruits side by side.
3. **Service tints:** a soft ground plus a matching icon badge for each service.
   - Pérdida de peso → `bg-weight` / `svc-weight` (peach)
   - Tratamiento médico → `bg-medical` / `svc-medical` (blue-grey, calm register, no food)
   - Nutrición deportiva → `bg-sport` / `svc-sport` (butter yellow)
   - Nutrición clínica → `bg-clinic` / `svc-clinic` (pale sage)
   - Blog article categories reuse the same mapping, with hábitos → `bg-weight`.
4. **Dot marker:** `.sec-head h2::before`, a sun dot plus a brand dot. It appears only through `.sec-head`; do not add it anywhere else.
5. **Brand band** (`.cta-band.cta-band--v4.bg-brand`): at most one per page, always the closing section.

## 3. Colour

Colour is role-based. Values live only in the `PALETTE:BEGIN (salvia)` block of `css/tokens.css`, generated by `palettes/palettes.py`, which also checks 28 contrast pairs against WCAG AA.
- Roles: `--ink`, `--ink-2`, `--paper`, `--surface`, `--surface-2`, `--brand`, `--brand-hover`, `--brand-tint`, `--brand-soft`, `--footer`, `--accent`, `--accent-tint`, `--sun`, `--placeholder`, `--t-weight`, `--t-medical`, `--t-sport`, `--t-clinic`, plus `--ink-rgb` and `--brand-rgb`.
- Hard rules:
  - `--sun` is a fill only: the featured-plan button, protocol numbers, the dot marker and ticks on brand. Never use it as text on light grounds.
  - `--accent` is text or icon only on paper and on its tints (provisional badges, note icons).
  - Primary button = brand fill with paper text. On the brand band it inverts to paper fill with brand text. In the featured plan it is sun fill with ink text.
  - Section grounds alternate so that two adjacent sections never share a ground; the section right after a tinted hero is `bg-paper`.
- The other palettes (`oliva`, `petroleo`) stay in `palettes/` as a reversible fallback. Changing palette means running the script and pasting the block, with no component changes.

## 4. Typography

- Headings: **Fraunces**, self-hosted in subset form.
  - `fonts/fraunces-web.woff2`: opsz 24–96, wght 450–600, SOFT fixed at 50. Weight 560.
  - `fonts/fraunces-italic-web.woff2`: opsz 24–96, wght 450. Used for `<em>` accents only.
- Body and UI: **Public Sans** (`fonts/public-sans-web.woff2`, wght 400–700). Body is 17 px with line-height 1.65 and a measure of at most 68ch.
- The article body uses the serif (`.article-body.prose`).
- Total web fonts weigh 84 KB. Preload `fraunces-web` and `public-sans-web`; do not preload the italic.
- OG images use static TTFs: `fonts/fraunces-og-560.ttf`, `fraunces-og-italic-450.ttf`, `public-sans-og-500.ttf` and `public-sans-og-700.ttf`.
- Prices and step numbers use tabular figures.
- Never: text below 14 px, all-caps labels, letter-spaced eyebrows, or gradient text.

## 5. Layout and responsive

- Container 72rem; gutter is fluid. Breakpoints are 600, 900 and 1024 only.
- Two-column grids collapse to one column below 900 px:
  - `hero__grid` 7/5
  - `page-hero__grid` 7/5
  - `split--5-7`
  - `profile`
  - `cta-band__inner`
- Service rows: on mobile the description goes on its own full-width line and the food thumbnail is hidden.
- Home hero on mobile: the photo stage first, then the credentials card overlapping its bottom edge (`margin-top: -3rem`).
- Protocol: 5 columns at 900 px and up; below that, a vertical rail with a connecting line.
- Touch targets are at least 44 px. At 360 px there is no horizontal scroll.

## 8. Motion

Motion is used for state feedback and one entrance only: button colour, row hover (tint plus a fruit tilt), the FAQ icon rotation, the price swap and the hero settle. Durations are 120–260 ms; the settle takes 700 ms. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
