## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro (static output, content collections for the blog), deployable to GitHub Pages for prototyping and to any static/edge host later. Chosen by the planner (see SPEC DEC-03).

## Users

- People in Spain who want to lose weight / reduce fat / improve body composition and have tried "dietas milagro" before. Primary acquisition target.
- People considering weight-loss with medical (pharmacological, incl. GLP-1 pathway) support who need reassurance that it is supervised by a physician and paired with nutrition/habits.
- Recreational and competitive athletes (strength, endurance, other) wanting muscle gain, performance, recovery.
- People with clinical conditions (digestive, allergies/intolerances, dyslipidemia, hypertension, other metabolic) wanting nutrition follow-up.
- Situation: mostly on a phone, often arriving from Instagram/WhatsApp shares or search; decide whether to message Daniel.

## Product Purpose

Inform prospective clients and convert them into a first contact (WhatsApp / form) for 100% online nutrition follow-up. Secondary: educational blog.

## Positioning

Online nutritionist with clinical background (3+ years obesity / bariatric surgery consult) and 8+ years online with athletes, university master's lecturer, evidence-based and explicitly against miracle diets; offers an optional track coordinated with a physician who may prescribe authorised medication.

## Operating Context

- Process: initial assessment → personalised plan (Excel 365 with recipes and menu) → weekly check-in + WhatsApp → monthly ~20-minute video call → adjustments.
- Programs: A Nutritional follow-up (180 € / 2 m, 320 € / 4 m, 450 € / 6 m). B Nutrition + medical treatment (280 € / 2 m, 420 € / 4 m, 550 € / 6 m). Medication never included. All prices provisional.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact channels (WhatsApp number, email, booking, payment) are NOT confirmed. No real form backend.
- Legal pages are placeholders.
- Medical claims must stay within Spanish rules: no medicine names, no promotion, prescription belongs to the physician.
- Spanish only (es_ES).

## Brand Commitments

Name "Daniel Clemente · Nutricionista". No logo exists. Existing photography is AI-generated placeholder (hero + portrait) and must be replaced by real photos.

## Evidence on Hand

Credentials (UEM degree, FP superior dietética, UCAM master), experience years, teaching role. No testimonials, no client numbers, no press, no before/after. These must never be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Honesty over hype: no promises of results, no miracle language.
2. Clarity of limits: what the nutritionist does vs what the physician does.
3. Low friction to first contact from a phone.
4. Evidence-based, warm, human voice (first person, tú).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body-image sensitive: no before/after, no scales, no measuring tapes around waists, no shame imagery. Reduced motion respected.
