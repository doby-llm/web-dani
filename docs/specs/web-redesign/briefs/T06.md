> v4 brief (2026-11-17). Supersedes the v2 version; DESIGN.md v4 + mockups are the visual authority.

## Goal
Three pages that carry the commercial and medical limits of the offer: `/perdida-de-peso/tratamiento-medico/` (calm register, no food), `/programas/` (both plans with full price tables and the conditions block), and `/como-funciona/` (five-step protocol). Every disclaimer goes through `Note`. The medication-not-included sentence must be visible on the medical and programas pages (row L1).
