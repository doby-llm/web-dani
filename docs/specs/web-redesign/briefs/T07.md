## Planner notes (contradictions)
1. **`LegalPage` props.** The spec lists only `copyKey`. The breadcrumb needs a label, so the brief adds `crumb`. Ruling needed; the alternative is a per-page breadcrumb outside the component.
2. **FAQ breadcrumb label** is taken from the text before " | " in `preguntas-frecuentes.json` `meta.title`. That derivation is fragile; a microcopy key would be cleaner.
3. **Legal description length.** SPEC §4.4 says legal descriptions are one line from their lead. The `privacidad` lead is about 245 characters, above the 155 limit. Needs a ruling in `seo.json` (T10).
4. **Legal action bar.** The legal mockup has no `data-action-bar-trigger`, so the bar never shows there. R10 says to put the trigger on the lead. The brief follows the mockup. Ruling needed.
5. **Sobre mí h2.** The mockup's h2 is "Formación documentada", which no copy key holds. The brief uses `formacion.heading`, so "Formación" appears as both the h2 and the first CV row label. A microcopy key is needed if the mockup wording is wanted.
6. **Sobre mí still objects.** The mockup uses grapefruit + lemon-c; the earlier brief had lemon-a + avocado. The mockup is followed (its band is avocado + lemon-a). Confirm.
7. **Portrait caption.** The mockup caption is "Evidencia + cercanía". Microcopy `tag.portrait` is "Evidencia · + cercanía". The brief uses the microcopy string as written. Confirm, or add a key.
8. **Legal h1 accent** (`n=2`) is taken from the aviso-legal mockup and applied to all three legal pages. Confirm the words for privacidad and cookies.
9. **Unused legal hero CTAs.** The legal copy has `hero_ctas` that the mockup does not show. Listed for the T11 allowlist.
